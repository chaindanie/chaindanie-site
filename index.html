About

Chain Danie is an independent artist creating emotionally driven music that blends cultures, languages, and sound. His work moves between alternative-indie pop, acoustic, lo-fi, and atmospheric elements, exploring themes of identity, connection, and everything in between. Each project reflects a growing artistic journey—raw, evolving, and shaped by multiple influences across worlds.
