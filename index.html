About

Chain Danie is a multilingual independent artist crafting emotionally-driven music across cultures and languages.

Born between worlds and shaped by Malay, Turkish, and Western influences, his sound moves between indie pop, acoustic, lo-fi, and atmospheric textures—blurring borders both sonically and lyrically.

Each release reflects an evolving journey through identity, memory, and connection, where no single language fully contains the story.
