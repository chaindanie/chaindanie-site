About

Chain Danie is an independent singer-songwriter and producer whose music explores memory, identity, love, and the spaces people leave behind. Writing across English, Malay, Turkish, and German, his work reflects a cross-cultural perspective shaped by family heritage, travel, and experiences lived between countries.

His songs often move between what was, what could have been, and what remains after a season ends. Whether documenting long-distance connections, imagined futures, or questions of belonging, Chain Danie approaches songwriting with emotional honesty and quiet introspection.

Across releases such as Melancholica, Balada Jiwa, Diantara, and Pocket Room Cuts Vol. 1, he blends intimate storytelling with acoustic textures, alternative pop influences, and multilingual lyricism.
